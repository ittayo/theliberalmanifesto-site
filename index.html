About

The short version

I've spent my career solving problems in code — trained early on Visual Basic and Delphi, later mentored directly by Robert "Uncle Bob" Martin in Clean Code and SOLID, and I still think of myself as a problem-solver first and a software engineer second. That habit of mind didn't stay inside the IDE.

Outside of work I write — a philosophical memoir under my own name, romance fiction under the pen name Katie A. Novak, essays on Medium, and The Liberal Manifesto. This site is the place I've meant to put all of it for a long time. Consider this page one — it's a first draft, built to react to and correct, not a finished monument.

A Declaration

The Liberal Manifesto

One of the only wholly serious things I've published. Grounded in Popper's paradox of tolerance — "unlimited tolerance must lead to the disappearance of tolerance" — it sets out principles of community over tribalism, commitment to facts, conditional tolerance, good-faith dialogue, and individual freedom with reciprocity. A standalone page, in Hebrew and English.

I met a man who wasn't there
He wasn't there again today

Go away, go away, and please don't slam the door... (slam!)
