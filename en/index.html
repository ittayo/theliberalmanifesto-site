The Liberal Manifesto

“Unlimited tolerance must lead to the disappearance of tolerance.”
— Karl Popper, 1945

We believe in community. Not the nation, not the tribe, not the party — but the neighborhood of people who agree to live together by shared rules of decency.

We deal in facts. Not because we are always right, but because we are willing to be corrected. The moment you stop being correctable — you leave the community.

We tolerate everything except the destruction of tolerance itself. This is not a contradiction. It is the only way tolerance survives.

We talk to those who talk in good faith. We do not debate liars. Engaging a liar is not open-mindedness — it is lending him your platform.

Disagree with us — that's fine. Deceive us, bully us, corrupt the system — and you lose your seat at the table. The door remains open. But the seat is earned.

We do not initiate violence. We do not romanticize it. But we do not confuse non-violence with passivity. Withdrawal of community is not violence. It is justice.

We seek power to protect the community, not to own it. The moment we use power to silence rather than protect — we become what we fight.

We protect your right to be different — in belief, identity, lifestyle, conviction. The community does not demand conformity. It demands only one thing: that you extend to others the same freedom you claim for yourself.

“Love thy neighbor as yourself. I am the Lord.”
— Leviticus 19:18
